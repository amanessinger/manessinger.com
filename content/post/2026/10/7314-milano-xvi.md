I had tagged this image as part of the church, but that's not correct. It looks more like the entrance to a private
patio. Looking at the 3D model of Milano on Google Maps for private patios very close to Santa Maria delle Grazie, I
could easily identify Casa degli Atellani, just across the street. It's the only palace remaining here, and it belonged
to [Duke Ludovico Sforza](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ludovico_Sforza). About the same time as the church and close in
style, so my mistake is not entirely embarrassing :grin:
