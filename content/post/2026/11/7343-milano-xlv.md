I came here after the restoration of most of the facade, but before restoration of the inside. This was five years ogo.
Recent images of the inside on Google Maps are no different. There's also still some scaffolding on the roof. Obviously
restoring such massive cathedrals takes lots of time and money.

But can't they just pour more money into the process? Well, last time I was there, I arrived by car, spent a day in
Milano, took a lot of images, and then left. Money spent? The entrance fee for Leonardo, some coffees, maybe a glass of
wine, some sandwiches and ... nothing. Even more important: nothing was spent on the Duomo. I suppose, most of the
tourists do the same. And there you have it: that's why everything's so slow.
