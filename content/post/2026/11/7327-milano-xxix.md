Do I hunt for perfect light home in Vienna? I certainly could, but I don't. For me, photography is mainly a way of
processing the moment, regardless of where I am and of time of day. It's a way of collecting the substance of future
memories. It's also not the production of the memories. That really is post-processing. 

It's unfortunate that I am so far behind finishing my images, but that can't be helped either. I still have a job, and
after retirement, I'll have hobbies.

I write this on October 31<sup>st</sup>, 2026. What you see is from September 3<sup>rd</sup>, 2021. I'm still working on
finishing the images from that week in northern Italy. Today I have 147 finished images in sequence, and they still
don't cover noon of September 5<sup>th</sup>, 2021. It might take this blog ten or twelve months to reach the first
image of 2022 :grin:
